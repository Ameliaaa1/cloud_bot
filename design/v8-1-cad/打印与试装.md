# CloudBot V8.1 Printing and Fit Testing

V8.1 retains the [V8 integrated rail chassis](../v8-cad/组装与打印.md) and updates the dual-latch lid and matching tank. The [Fusion inspection report](validation.json) records 4 printable bodies and no listed assembly interferences. Physical latch operation, access for insertion and removal, and sealing still require fit-test validation.

Print `CloudBot-V8.1-dry-plate.stl` as one plate, then remove the four parts separately. To update only the dual-latch design, print the revised tank and lid together using `CloudBot-V8.1-tank-and-cap.stl`. Alternatively, import the individual STL files numbered 01–04. Use millimeters, preserve the orientations in the files, and slice with settings for the actual printer and material.

## Support Settings

| Part | Orientation and supports |
| --- | --- |
| 01 Integrated chassis | Place the large flat face on the bed and start with supports disabled. Check that the rails, cable-tie holes, and mist opening remain clear. |
| 02 Tank | Place the base on the bed with the opening facing up. The sloped floor expands outward layer by layer, and the tops of the side latch slots form short bridges of approximately 7 mm. Start with supports disabled. Use layer preview to check the bridge paths over both side slots. |
| 03 Dual-latch lid | Place the smooth face on the bed with the latches facing up; start with supports disabled. Inspect both short hooks closely in layer preview. |
| 04 Camera bracket | Place the slider's bottom face on the bed with the backing plate facing up. If the preview shows that the ledges extending to either side of the backing plate's base need support, add it locally only beneath these overhanging side ledges, ensuring it can be gripped and removed from outside. Keep the small cable-tie windows and rail slots free of support. |

If the slicer treats the complete-plate STL as one object, use “Split to objects” or import individual STL files to configure supports separately for part 04. After slicing, inspect layer preview to ensure no supports extend into the tank cavity, latch slots, or chassis holes. If long unsupported overhanging paths appear, first use painted supports to mark contact surfaces accessible from outside.

## Fit-Test Sequence

1. Align the lid's locating flange with the tank opening and press downward, confirming that both hooks engage their slots. To remove the lid, gently move both latch arms outward, then lift the lid.
2. Slide the lidded tank into the chassis from the water-path end. Check that the 6 mm tank-floor hole connects to the 16 mm chassis hole and that the latches can be operated from outside.
3. Start with a dry fit test. Once the actual atomizer and seals are matched, leak-test the water path separately from the electronics before assembling the complete unit.
