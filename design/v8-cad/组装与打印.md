# CloudBot V8 Complete Prototype

`CloudBot-V8.f3d` is the complete Fusion assembly. The default view exposes the internal components. Enable **91 Cloud diffuser envelope** and **92 Flowerpot reference** in the left-hand browser to see the cover and flowerpot. A flowerpot approximately 120 mm in diameter provides a scale reference. The cloud cover consists of a hand-bendable wire frame and a translucent outer cover.

| Printed part | Position and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| 01 Integrated chassis | Each end has rails and a latch stop. LED-strip supports are integrated into the sides, and a central barrier separates the tank from the camera. A 16 mm diameter through-opening is reserved beneath the tank. |
| 02 Tank | Slides in from the end with the through-opening. It has an integrated latch and handle; press the latch to withdraw it. The 6 mm diameter hole in its floor is coaxial with the chassis opening. The geometric internal cavity is approximately 9.6 mL. |
| 03 Lid | Turn it over to place it on the tank. An internal tongue aids alignment, and an external handle aids lifting. This is currently a dry-fit locating part; approximately 0.35 mm between the flange and tank rim is reserved for a future sealing solution. |
| 04 Camera tray | Slides in from the opposite end of the chassis. The camera board rests against the upright backing plate, with cable ties threaded through the side holes. Loosen cables before withdrawing the tray. |

Both sliding parts use the R2 sample's 0.25 mm lateral latch clearance. Once fully inserted, pull gently to check engagement. To remove, press the side latch and pull out. Check the actual ribbon cable and connector positions before securing the board through the tray's side holes. The LED-support holes are beside the strip; cable ties return beneath the support without passing through other plates.

Two stainless-steel stakes, 3 mm in diameter and approximately 145 mm long, pass through 3.5 mm diameter chassis holes spaced 64 mm apart. Shaft collars beneath the plate can provide stops during fit testing; use ballast to check pull-out and wobble first. Tie the thin aluminum-wire cloud frame to the chassis through the four side holes. Keep the cover removable and leave openings for the lens, latches, and mist outlet. The flowerpot, stakes, wire, and electronics are assembly reference parts.

Import `CloudBot-V8-dry-plate.stl` in millimeters at 100% scale to print all four parts together, or select the corresponding numbered individual STL files. In the slicer, inspect both rails, the through-opening, and cable-tie holes layer by layer. The raised side wings at both ends of the camera tray may need supports that can be removed from outside.

This iteration is for dry assembly. The atomizer module has not been selected; the modeled atomizer disk is a dimensional placeholder. Lid clamping and water-path sealing have not been physically tested either. Use ballast with the locating lid and printed tank to verify latches and stability first. Once the actual atomizer is available, complete the wet-area interfaces and leak-test them separately, away from electronics.
